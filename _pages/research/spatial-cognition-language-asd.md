### Overview
While Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is clinically defined by social communication differences and restricted, repetitive behaviours, cognitive abilities across the spectrum remain highly heterogeneous. By administering a comprehensive battery of tasks, this study investigated how sub-domains of spatial cognition and structural language differentiate autistic and neurotypical individuals using data-driven Elastic Net regression modelling. Identified spatial verbal memory as a key differentiating predictor and explored cross-system cognitive integration differences in ASD.

### Reflection
I'm more than happy to work on a Neurodiversity study! My experience on this project was multifaceted. Working alongside a statistics peer student expanded my analytical toolkit through exposure to unsupervised machine learning algorithms, while discussions with clinical practitioners offered valuable real-world context on childhood autism diagnosis and current ADOS-2 implementation. Most strikingly, I realised how closely our task measures were tied to the participants' individual identities and profiles, a rich connection that was much less apparent in my earlier studies.
